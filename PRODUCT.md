# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Java 21 + Spring Boot modular monolith; server-rendered Thymeleaf pages with custom CSS, plain JavaScript, Leaflet maps, Chart.js charts, Spring WebSocket/STOMP for live updates, PostgreSQL. No Node.js build step.

## Users

- **Dispatchers** (primary): work in a dispatch office with the control tower open all day on a large monitor, glancing at it constantly and acting when something goes wrong. Their job is to keep every active delivery on schedule and to resolve disruptions quickly.
- **Admins**: maintain vehicles, drivers, warehouses, users, and risk-rule settings.
- **Drivers**: use a phone-sized page during a trip to see stops, confirm arrivals, complete deliveries with proof, and report problems.
- **Recipients / public**: look up a shipment by tracking number and PIN code.
- **Portfolio viewers**: people evaluating the project from screenshots, a demo video, or a live walkthrough.

## Product Purpose

Fleet Operations Intelligence Platform: a logistics control tower that monitors a delivery fleet in real time, detects delivery risk with transparent rules, and helps dispatchers take corrective action. Success means a dispatcher can see at a glance what is going wrong, why, and what to do, and every decision is recorded.

## Positioning

Not a tracking map and not an ML black box: a continuous monitoring and decision-support system whose risk scores are rule-based and always explained ("+30 projected 25 min late, +20 location stale 6 min"), tied directly to an incident workflow and an audit trail.

## Operating Context

- Demo region: Punjab, India. Warehouses in Ludhiana, Amritsar, Jalandhar, Patiala, Bathinda, Mohali; deliveries to surrounding towns.
- Vehicle movement comes from a simulator on a shared simulation clock (1×–60×), following cached road routes.
- Display time zone Asia/Kolkata.
- Portfolio project with no fixed deadline; built phase by phase per `final_plan.md`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Modules: control tower, tracking & simulation, dispatch planning & routes, ETA & risk engine, incidents & corrective action, master data, driver app, public tracking, in-app notifications, analytics, security & audit, demo data seeder.
- Roles: Admin, Dispatcher, Driver; public tracking is anonymous.
- Low local footprint: no Docker, no self-hosted routing; public OSRM called once per route plan and cached.
- No machine learning; risk is a rule-based indicator, never presented as a probability.

## Evidence on Hand

No real customers, fleet data, logos or photography. All operational data is synthetic, generated by the demo seeder, and must be labelled as demo data where a viewer could mistake it for real operations. No invented clients, metrics or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Exceptions first: the screen leads with what needs attention, not with everything that is fine.
2. Every number is explainable and traceable to the trips, rules and actions behind it.
3. One shared state: map, incidents, trip records and analytics always agree.
4. A dispatcher's action is never lost: every change is attributed and audited.
5. Credible as a daily tool and memorable at first sight, in equal measure.

## Brand Commitments

- Must not look like a sci-fi or hacker "mission control" HUD (black screen, neon glow).
